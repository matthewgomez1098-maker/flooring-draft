How it goes

Four steps, no surprises.

The same sequence on every job, from a cracked vent boot to a full tear-off.

You call

A person picks up. We ask what rooms, roughly what size, and what is on the floor now, because that is most of the quote.

We measure and test

We measure for real, take moisture readings of the slab or subfloor, and check how flat it is. You see those numbers.

Written quote

Material, labour, prep and disposal listed separately, so you can see what the prep actually costs instead of guessing.

Install and walk-through

We protect what stays, keep the dust down, and walk the finished floor with you before the last payment.

Questions

The ones we get asked on every estimate.

Can I put hardwood over a concrete slab?

Engineered hardwood, yes, with a moisture barrier and after the slab tests dry. Solid hardwood over slab is asking for trouble in this climate, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you a callback. We take the moisture reading first and show it to you.

Why is the prep more expensive than the flooring?

Because flat and dry is what makes a floor last, and getting there can mean grinding, levelling compound or subfloor repair. We list prep separately on the quote so you can see it rather than finding it as a surprise mid-job.

Do I have to move the furniture?

Tell us and we will handle it. Moving it yourself brings the price down, so if you would rather do that, do. What we will not do is work around a full room and leave you a floor with a patch under the couch.

How long before I can walk on it?

Floating floors, same day. Glue-down, usually twenty-four hours. Tile, twenty-four to forty-eight before grout and light traffic after that. Epoxy depends on the system and we will give you the number before we start.
